# Audio design

All music and sound effects are **original** and generated by code: `assets_source/gen_audio.py` synthesizes the
instruments and writes genuine FastTracker II modules (`.xm`, through `assets_source/audio/xm.py`) and WAV sound
effects. Butano's maxmod backend converts them into the ROM's soundbank. Nothing is sampled from existing
recordings.

## Music

Thirteen tracks, all looping except the two jingles:

| Track (gallery title) | Tempo | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Crown of Six | 100 BPM | title screen, menus, opening cinematic, prologue |
| The Six Roads | 116 BPM | world map |
| Emerald Canopy | 100 BPM, 3/4 | the Verdant March (kingdom 1), the tutorial |
| Iron and Ember | 150 BPM | the Inferno Bastion (kingdom 2) |
| Mirror of Winter | 80 BPM, 3/4 | the Frozen Court (kingdom 3) |
| Forbidden Pages | 72 BPM | the Umbral Archive (kingdom 4), challenges |
| Thunder Over Stone | 156 BPM | the Storm Spire (kingdom 5) |
| The Floating Crown | 88 BPM | the Crown Nexus (kingdom 6) |
| Arcane Gambit | 132 BPM | quick matches (Classic and Arcane) |
| The Ruler's Gambit | 146 BPM | boss encounters, the boss gauntlet |
| Checkmate Fanfare | 120 BPM | victory (jingle) |
| The Fallen King | 66 BPM | defeat (jingle) |
| Six Hands, One Crown | 84 BPM | epilogue, ending cinematic, credits |

The themes share motifs. The **Crown motif** (D minor: D-A-D-F-E, D-C-A-G-A) opens the title theme and returns
transformed: in major on the world map, augmented and choral in the Crown Nexus, as a rhythm in the boss battle,
and in major and slow in the ending. Every background theme has at least two contrasting sections and loops at
the start of a section, so the loop point is musical.

### Format and instruments

* FastTracker II XM modules, 8 channels, written by our own XM writer (patterns, instruments with volume
  envelopes, fade-out and vibrato). `tools/check_xm.py` re-parses every module independently: header, order
  table, every pattern decoded to exactly rows x channels cells with valid notes and instrument numbers, and
  instrument and sample sizes consistent with the file.
* Fifteen synthesized instruments, each a single-cycle waveform built from harmonics, with a volume envelope:
  bell, strings, bass, pluck, flute, brass, choir, organ and lead; and six one-shot drums (kick, snare, closed and
  open hi-hat, tom, crash) synthesized from noise and pitch sweeps.
* Harmony is written as chord symbols and voiced automatically (smooth voice leading inside a fixed register).

## Sound effects

Twenty-three effects, mono 8-bit WAV at 16 kHz, synthesized from tone sweeps (sine, square, triangle, saw), noise
bursts and bell tones:

| Group | Effects |
|---|---|
| Interface | cursor, menu move, menu select, menu cancel, invalid action |
| Chess | piece select, piece move, capture, check, checkmate (a D minor chord over a falling bass), stalemate, promotion |
| Spells | Shadow Step (smoky noise), Phase Shift, Ghost Walk, Castle Swap (a grinding swap and a bell), Royal Command (two brass calls), Royal Escape (a rising sweep and a sparkle) |
| Events | achievement (a bright arpeggio), unlock, boss appearance (a low rumble), victory, defeat |

Each ability has its own sound, so a spell can be recognized by ear.

## Playback

`src/cl_audio.cpp` handles every request:

* Music and sound can each be switched off, with volumes from 0 to 10 (Options and the pause menu settings,
  saved in the profile). Music plays at up to 75% volume to leave headroom for the effects.
* While music is off, the wanted track is remembered, so turning it on again resumes the right theme.
* The same effect starts at most once every 3 frames, and at most 3 new effects start in one frame, so bursts
  of events never distort.
* In a duel the capture sound plays at the moment of impact; check, checkmate and promotion sounds wait until the
  duel is over.

## Verification

The `audio` emulator scenario (`tests/emu/audio.txt`) records the console's audio output. It checks that music
plays (measured signal level) and follows the scenes (title, world map, battle), that switching music off silences
the game and switching it on brings the theme back, and that moves and captures make their sounds. During
development, pitch-class analysis of the captured audio confirmed the keys (the title in D minor, the world map in
B flat major).
